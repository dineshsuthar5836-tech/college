Policies & Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

A disciplined environment is the foundation of excellence. All students are expected to uphold these standards at all times.

Attendance Policy

  • Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory in all courses.
  • Students below 75% may be detained from examinations.
  • Medical leaves require a certified doctor's certificate.
  • Monthly attendance reports are communicated to parents/guardians.

Discipline & Conduct

  • Respectful behavior with faculty, staff, and fellow students is mandatory.
  • Use of mobile phones inside classrooms is strictly prohibited.
  • Violence, verbal abuse, or any misconduct leads to disciplinary action.
  • Damage to college property is a punishable offence.

Dress Code

  • Students must dress modestly in accordance with Indian cultural values.
  • College ID cards must be visibly worn on campus at all times.
  • Torn, offensive, or extremely casual clothing is not permitted.
  • Formal attire preferred during examinations and official events.

Examination Rules

  • Students must report at least 15 minutes before scheduled exam time.
  • Unfair means (copying) is a serious offence and may lead to expulsion.
  • Valid college ID card must be presented at all examinations.
  • Question papers and answer sheets are college property and must not be taken out.

Anti-Ragging Policy

  • Ragging in any form, physical, verbal, or digital, is strictly prohibited.
  • Offenders face immediate suspension and legal action as per UGC norms.
  • An active Anti-Ragging Committee is accessible to all students.
  • Incidents can be reported confidentially to the committee or helpline.

Campus Etiquette

  • Keep the campus clean; always use designated dustbins.
  • Smoking, alcohol, and narcotics are strictly banned on campus.
  • Silence must be maintained in library and examination zones.
  • Vehicles must be parked only in designated parking areas.
